On your computer (Outlook, Outlook on the web, Mac)

  1. Open your Computer link. The page shows only your signature.
  2. Click anywhere on the page, select everything (Ctrl + A / Cmd + A) and copy (Ctrl + C / Cmd + C).
  3. New Outlook / Outlook on the web: gear icon → Account → Signatures → + New signature, give it a name and paste (Ctrl + V).
  4. Classic Outlook: File → Options → Mail → Signatures… → New, then paste. Mac: Outlook → Settings → Signatures → +, then paste.
  5. Choose it as the default for New messages and Replies/forwards, and save.

On your phone (Outlook for iPhone / Android)

  1. Open this email on your phone and tap your Phone link. It opens in your browser and shows only your signature.
  2. Press and hold on the signature, tap Select all, then Copy.
  3. Open Outlook, tap your profile picture or initial (top left), then the gear icon (Settings).
  4. Tap Signature. If you have several accounts, pick your A2Z account (on iPhone, first turn on Per Account Signature).
  5. Delete the default text, press and hold inside the box and tap Paste.
  6. Save: on Android tap ✓; on iPhone just go back, it saves automatically.

If the image doesn't show on your phone: tap your Image link, press and hold the image and save it (Save to Photos on iPhone). Then in Outlook → Signature, put the cursor at the very beginning, tap the image icon and choose the saved image. Keep the details and links underneath and save.
