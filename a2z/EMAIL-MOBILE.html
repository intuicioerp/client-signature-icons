How to set it up (5 minutes, on your phone)

  1. Open this email on your phone and tap your Mobile signature link. It opens in your browser and shows only your signature.
  2. Press and hold on the signature, tap Select all, then Copy.
  3. Open Outlook, tap your profile picture or initial (top left), then the gear icon (Settings).
  4. Tap Signature. If you have several accounts, pick your A2Z account (on iPhone, first turn on Per Account Signature).
  5. Delete the default text, press and hold inside the box and tap Paste.
  6. Save: on Android tap ✓; on iPhone just go back, it saves automatically.

Test it

Send yourself an email from your phone and open it: the image and the links should be there (tap the email and website to check them).

If the image doesn't show

  1. Tap your Image link, press and hold the image and choose Download image (Save to Photos on iPhone).
  2. Go back to Outlook → gear icon → Signature, put the cursor at the very beginning and tap the image icon → choose the image you just saved.
  3. Keep the rest of the signature (details and links) underneath and save.

On your computer, keep using your full desktop signature as before.
